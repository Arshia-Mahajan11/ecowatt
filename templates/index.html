    About the data

    • UCI Appliances Energy Prediction dataset: real public research data at 10-minute intervals from a monitored low-energy building.
    • It is not a live household feed and not Indian household data.
    • The ₹{{ tariff }}/kWh tariff is illustrative. Replace it with a real slab rate for personal estimates.
    • The dataset has no AC column, so AC load is not made up. Only measured lighting is separated out; the rest is labelled Other appliances.

Random Forest + Isolation Forest
PROBLEM

Indian households and small businesses rarely know where their electricity goes or when demand quietly turns unusual — bills arrive as a surprise, not a warning.

OUR APPROACH

EcoWatt trains real machine-learning models — a Random Forest regressor and an Isolation Forest anomaly detector — on 19,735 genuine appliance-energy measurements to forecast usage and flag abnormal patterns before they show up on the bill.

IMPACT

Early anomaly warnings + a transparent savings simulator help nudge everyday behaviour change — supporting affordable, sustainable energy use (aligned with SDG 7 and SDG 12).
